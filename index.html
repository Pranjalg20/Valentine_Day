To My Potato,

I would absolutely give you the moon
but it’s not mine to give

I would plant flowers on your pillow
but that’s not where flowers want to live

I would learn to speak to birds
and ask them to sing your favourite song
but bird language is not on duolingo
and I have a much bigger tongue

I would break off a piece of the star
to make sure you always have light
but my hands are only flesh
and can’t extend to that height

I would capture the ocean’s waves
and place them gently at your feet,
but the tide has its own rhythm,
a song I cannot repeat.

I would gather echoes of laughter
from every joyous place you’ve been,
but sound is meant to wander,
not be held deep within.

I would chase the fleeting sunset
and bottle up its golden hue,
but colors fade without the sky—
they’re not meant to be subdued.

I would give you all these wonders,
but they’re not mine to bestow.
Instead, I’ll stand beside you,
so you never walk alone.

I love you so much


your emo crybaby
